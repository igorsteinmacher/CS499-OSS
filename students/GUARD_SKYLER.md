<h1>Salad Theory Report</h1>

**Title:** "Salad Theory: A comprehensive, precise, and pedantic branch of set theory pertaining to food categorization."<br>
**Venue:** GitHub<br>
**Pages:** 6<br>
**Link:** https://saladtheory.github.io/<br>

This paper outlines the details of Salad Theory, a theory of food categorization which posits that all foods are salads, hyper-salads, or saladoids. The paper also describes the Hyper-Salad Space Standard Projection, a useful projection of Hyper-Salad Space onto the three dimensions of soupiness, ingredient entropy, and arrangement entropy.
