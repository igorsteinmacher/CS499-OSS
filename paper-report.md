# Paper Report

## Paper Details

- **Title**: An empirical investigation on the challenges faced by women in the software industry: A case study
- **Venue**: ICSE-SEIS '22: Proceedings of the 2022 ACM/IEEE 48th International Conference on Software Engineering: Software Engineering in Society
- **Number of Pages**: 12 pages (pp. 24–35)
- **Link to the paper online**: https://dl.acm.org/doi/epdf/10.1145/3510458.3513018

## Summary

This paper presents an exploratory case study conducted at Ericsson to investigate the challenges faced by women in global software development teams. By surveying 94 women practitioners, the authors identified key socio-cultural and professional hurdles that lead to women leaving tech companies, alongside strategies and practices to mitigate these challenges.
